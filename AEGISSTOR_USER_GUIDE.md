# AEGISSTOR — User Guide

## 1. Navigation (11 Sections)
AEGISSTOR organizes all capabilities into 11 plain-English sections in the left sidebar:
1. **Overview**: Answers the 5 core questions at a glance — *Connected Storage*, *Storage Used*, *Needs Attention*, *Future Outlook*, and *Recommended Next Step* — plus capacity charts and info tooltips.
2. **Data Sources**: Configure & test storage/database connections, import CSV telemetry, and inspect Data Quality completeness.
3. **Storage**: Browse Storage Systems, Storage Topology, and individual Device Details.
4. **Monitoring**: View Live Monitoring streams, Performance Trends (IOPS, latency, throughput), and Workload Patterns.
5. **Predictions**: Inspect Capacity Forecasts (`Q10 / Q50 / Q90`), Forecasting Models, and Forecast Accuracy metrics.
6. **Risk and Alerts**: Review Capacity Risk timelines and active Alerts.
7. **Optimisation**: Explore Optimisation candidates and plain-English Recommendations.
8. **Digital Twin**: Run what-if simulations in **Digital Twin (Simulation)** before applying changes.
9. **Tasks and Approvals**: Track operational Tasks, Approvals, and Control Plane actions.
10. **Reports**: Generate Reports and inspect the Activity Log.
11. **Administration**: Manage Settings and Users & Roles (Administrator access).

## 2. Reading Metric Cards & Info Tooltips
Every key metric card displays the metric name, value, unit, last updated timestamp, a one-sentence explanation, and an info (`i`) tooltip separating everyday explanations from technical details.
