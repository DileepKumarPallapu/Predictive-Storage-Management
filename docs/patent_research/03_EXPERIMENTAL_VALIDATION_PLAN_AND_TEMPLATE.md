# AEGISSTOR — Experimental Validation Plan & Ablation Protocol (Research Task 4)

> **Evidence Rule**: Real-world multi-array enterprise field traces have **not** yet been evaluated over a 90-day live deployment. Controlled benchmark results generated by `backend/scripts/run_patent_ablation_study.py` reflect isolated chronological splits on deterministic test series (`PERIODIC_POSITIVE`, `ACCELERATING`, `DEGRADED_STALE`), and must be supplemented with multi-array enterprise storage logs before asserting production statistical superiority.

---

## 1. Chronological Validation & Leakage Prevention Protocol

To prevent lookahead bias, every candidate model and calibration stage is constrained to strictly temporal causal partitions:
1. **Supervised Lag Alignment**: Features at time $t$ use only observations at $\le t$ (`lag_1, lag_2, lag_6, lag_12, lag_24`, trailing rolling windows). Target is `used_gb` at $t+1$.
2. **Exogenous Zero-Fabrication Guard**: Optional IOPS, P95 latency, and throughput are retained only when $\ge 70\%$ of real observations are non-null and non-zero; missing sensors are excluded cleanly rather than zero-filled.
3. **3-Way Chronological Partition**:
   - **64% Train Split**: Model parameter fitting (`LGBMRegressor` / `SARIMAX`).
   - **16% Conformal Calibration Split**: Computing finite-sample Split Conformal absolute residual quantile $q_{0.80}$ (`conformal_q`) strictly out-of-train.
   - **20% Holdout Test Split**: Unseen final evaluation metrics (`MAE`, `RMSE`, `R^2`, `MASE`, `MAPE`, Pinball Loss, Empirical Coverage, Mean Interval Width).
4. **3-Fold Rolling-Origin Walk-Forward CV**: Expanding-origin splits over train+calib reporting out-of-sample stability (`rolling_mae`, `rolling_rmse`).

---

## 2. Seven Required Ablation Comparisons

1. **`SeasonalNaiveBenchmark`** vs **`LGBMRegressor`** vs **`SARIMAX`**: Establish baseline MAE, RMSE, MASE, and empirical 80% coverage on stationary periodic, accelerating quadratic, and noisy streams.
2. **Forecasts WITHOUT Telemetry Reliability Gate** (`uncertainty_multiplier = 1.0`, unblocked recommendations) vs **Forecasts WITH Reliability Gate** (`DataReliabilityEngine` score $R \to \gamma \ge 1.0$, `LOW`/`STALE` suppression): Measure impact on 80% coverage under 96h-stale & incomplete-metric degradation.
3. **Forecasts WITHOUT Workload Regime Detection** vs **Forecasts WITH `WorkloadRegimeEngine`**: Evaluate detection accuracy across `PERIODIC_SEASONAL`, `ACCELERATING_GROWTH`, `SUDDEN_BURST`, `DECLINING_CONSUMPTION`, `UNUSUAL_CONSUMPTION_CHANGE`, and `INSUFFICIENT_HISTORY`.
4. **Risk Assessments vs Observed Holdout Outcomes**: Evaluate empirical residual-CDF crossing probability calibrated via held-out **Brier Score**.
5. **Digital Twin & Constrained Decision Optimizer Determinism**: Verify hard-constraint rejection (`EXPAND_VOLUME` exceeding `budget_usd`, `MIGRATE_WORKLOAD` exceeding `max_latency_ms` or `max_transfer_gb`) and cost omission honesty (`Cost not provided` when unit costs omitted).
