# AEGISSTOR — Operator Guide

## 1. Operational Scope
Operators have full day-to-day operational capability across AEGISSTOR:
- **Storage & Telemetry**: Monitor Storage Systems, import CSV historical telemetry (`Import Data`), preview validation/duplicate checks, and evaluate Data Quality.
- **ML & Forecasting**: Trigger Champion-Challenger model training (when `>= 60` chronological samples exist), inspect Capacity Forecasts (`Q10/Q50/Q90`), and review Forecast Accuracy.
- **Risk, Digital Twin & Recommendations**: Inspect Capacity Risk runway, test mitigation actions in `Digital Twin (Simulation)`, and review Recommendations.
- **Tasks & Control Plane**: Create and update operational Tasks, comment with Administrators in real time, and submit Control Plane actions for approval when required.
